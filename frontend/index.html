About

About The Protocol

EcoTokens' mission is to create a transparent and trustworthy global carbon market. By leveraging blockchain technology, we eliminate greenwashing and provide a platform where every carbon credit is a verifiable, secure, and unique digital asset on the Ethereum network.

Our protocol is built on a robust, decentralized architecture using Solidity smart contracts for automated verification, tokenization (ERC-20 CCT tokens), and settlement. Role-based access control ensures only authorized Originators, Auditors, and Admins can perform sensitive operations.

Our vision is to become the leading standard for digital carbon assets. Future developments include a native governance token, advanced data analytics for environmental impact tracking, and partnerships with global ecological organizations to expand verified projects.

Features

Platform Features

🛡️

Immutable Records

All carbon credit data is permanently stored on the Ethereum blockchain, ensuring transparent and immutable proof of ownership and authenticity.

🔗

Smart Contract Automation

Automated processes for token minting, auditing, and trading via MetaMask reduce manual overhead and enhance transactional security and speed.

🌐

Decentralized Marketplace

A peer-to-peer marketplace for buying and selling Carbon Credit Tokens (CCT), eliminating intermediaries and ensuring fair, on-chain pricing.
