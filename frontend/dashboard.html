What is Retirement?

🌍 Real CO₂ Offset

Each CCT = 1 tonne of CO₂ offset. Retiring proves you permanently neutralised that carbon.

🔗 Blockchain Proof

The CreditRetired event is stored permanently on Ethereum — verifiable by anyone via Etherscan.

⚠️ Irreversible Action

Tokens are burned from the total supply. This cannot be undone.
